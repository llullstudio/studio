The Cuckoo Protocol

Something took Luke's place.

Spoilers: the Star Wars sequel trilogy, Rogue One, and Andor seasons 1 and 2.

In 1983, Luke Skywalker switches his lightsaber off over his father. In 2017, he switches it on over his sleeping nephew. Same name, same face, same actor. Not the same man.

There's a parasite that works this way. The cuckoo lays its egg in another bird's nest, its chick pushes the real eggs out, and the hosts keep feeding it. The video runs the sequel trilogy through that pattern, then asks a harder question: how did the same studio, under the same boss, also make Andor?

Five questions, head to head, and one test for whatever Star Wars makes next: does it deepen what it inherits, or spend it?
