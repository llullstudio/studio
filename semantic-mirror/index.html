Semantic Mirror

A framed piece, for a gallery or a home, made to hear the conversation around it and reflect the gist as an evolving abstract work. It starts blank. As people talk, forms grow, change and fade: organic when the talk is emotional, drier when it is factual. In silence it settles back to blank.

It reflects the gist, not the words. No text, no symbols. Each moment is also a record: a texture woven into the image carries an exact account of what was said, and a decoder can recover it from the picture alone. The art may express more than was said. The account never claims anything that wasn't.

In this demonstration the conversation is scripted and the piece renders it from prepared test data. Interpreting real, unscripted talk is the next step.
