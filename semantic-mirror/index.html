Semantic Mirror

Semantic Mirror is a framed artwork for a gallery or a home. It listens to the conversation around it and answers with abstract forms that grow, bristle, warm and fade. It starts blank, and in silence it returns to blank.

It reflects the gist, not the words. A dry remark drains the color; warmth swells; an insult grows thorns, which retract when it's taken back. Woven into each image is a compact record of what was said, so a snapshot of the piece can be decoded back into its gist. The art may express more than was said; the record never claims more.

It hears before it understands. A soft glow at the edge of the frame answers any voice at once; the forms change only when there is enough to go on. By design, whispers go unheard. It keeps no audio or transcript, doesn't tell voices apart, and records no names, numbers or other details that could identify anyone.

In this demonstration, two visitors test the piece until their game turns into something real. The conversation is scripted and the voices are generated; listening in real time is the next step.
